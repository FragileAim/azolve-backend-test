About Azolve

Business software built around how Indian businesses actually work.

We started Azolve because the tools small and growing businesses needed were either too generic, too expensive, or scattered across five different subscriptions.

Most business software is built for one specific job — a CRM that only does CRM, an invoicing tool that knows nothing about your customer history, an HR system that can't talk to either. For a small or growing business, that means juggling logins, re-entering the same customer details everywhere, and reconciling numbers across tools that were never meant to work together.

Azolve brings CRM, invoicing, HRM, finance and an AI copilot into one platform, built specifically with Indian business needs in mind — GST-compliant invoicing, UPI payments, WhatsApp-first communication, and pricing that doesn't punish you for growing your team.

We're a small team building for other small and growing teams. Every feature we ship is one we'd want to use ourselves running a business day to day.
