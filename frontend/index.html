FAQ

Questions we hear most.

Do I need to migrate all data at once?

No — most teams start with one module (usually Invoicing or CRM) and bring the rest over gradually. Azolve supports CSV import, and our team handles migration for Business and Enterprise plans.

Does it handle Indian GST and e-Invoicing?

Yes — GSTIN-based tax calculation, HSN/SAC codes, GSTR-1 export, and full NIC IRP integration for e-Invoice (IRN + QR code) are all built in. No third-party GST tool required.

Is the AI Copilot included or a paid add-on?

Included in Business and Enterprise — no extra charge. Starter plan has limited AI access. Full breakdown on the Pricing page.

Can I try it before committing?

Yes — start free with no credit card. You get 14 days full access to the Business plan. Downgrade, upgrade, or cancel anytime.

What support do you offer?

Starter: email support, 48h response. Business: priority chat + email, 4h response + onboarding call. Enterprise: dedicated account manager + phone support.
