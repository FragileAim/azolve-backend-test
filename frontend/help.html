Common questions

How does the free trial work?

Every plan starts with a free trial — no card required. You get full access to the modules on your chosen plan, and can upgrade or add modules any time from inside the app.

Can I add modules later without re-signing up?

Yes. Every plan shares the same underlying account, so adding CRM, HRM, or any other module later doesn't mean starting over — your existing data stays connected.

Is Azolve GST-compliant for Indian invoicing?

Yes — Invoicing includes GST-compliant invoices, e-invoicing with IRN and QR codes, and UPI payment links out of the box.

Can I import my existing customer and invoice data?

Yes, our team can help migrate customers, invoices and product catalogs from spreadsheets or most common accounting tools during onboarding.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You can export your data at any time. If you cancel, your data remains available for export for 30 days before removal.
