AI Co-Pilot

Ask anything about your business in plain English or Hindi. Co-Pilot understands invoices, clients, finances, and more.

⏰
"Show overdue invoices"
Lists every unpaid invoice past due date with customer & amount
🏆
"Top 5 clients this month"
Ranks customers by revenue with comparison vs last month
💰
"Cash flow this quarter"
Income vs expenses with monthly breakdown chart
📊
"Generate GST report"
GSTR-1 & GSTR-3B summary ready for filing
🌴
"Who's on leave today?"
Lists employees away with leave type & return date
💬
"WhatsApp all overdue"
Sends a polite reminder to every late-paying client
💡
Pro tip
Try asking complex multi-step questions like "Send a payment reminder to clients who haven't paid in 30+ days, then schedule a follow-up call next week." Co-Pilot can chain actions automatically.
